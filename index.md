# WhiteLister – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 4 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

WhiteLister is a Discord bot developed for the AvatarSMP Minecraft Bedrock community.

Its purpose is to manage player applications, whitelist administration, moderation records and server-related information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information WhiteLister collects, how it is used, how it is stored and what rights users have regarding their personal information.

By interacting with WhiteLister, you acknowledge that your information may be processed as described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

WhiteLister may collect and process the following information when you use its features:

**Discord account information**
- Discord user ID.
- Discord username and associated account information.
- Date your Discord account was created.
- Date you joined the AvatarSMP Discord server.

**Minecraft information**
- Minecraft Bedrock gamertag.
- Whitelist status.
- Information required to manage your server access.

**Application information**
- Submitted Minecraft gamertag.
- Confirmation that you have read and accepted the server rules.
- Confirmation of community participation.
- Application submission date.
- Application status and eligibility check results.

**Moderation information**
- Moderator responsible for reviewing an application.
- Approval, investigation or rejection decisions.
- Reasons provided for application rejection.
- Records of whitelist additions and removals.

**Activity information**
- Message activity counts used to help assess application eligibility.
- Relevant timestamps associated with activity tracking.

WhiteLister does not need to retain the actual contents of messages for basic message-count tracking.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected by WhiteLister is used for the following purposes:

- Processing and reviewing applications to join AvatarSMP.
- Checking basic application eligibility.
- Confirming Discord account and server membership information.
- Managing the Minecraft server allowlist.
- Notifying applicants about application decisions.
- Allowing authorised moderators to investigate applications.
- Maintaining records of moderation and whitelist actions.
- Preventing abuse of the application and whitelist systems.
- Supporting the security and administration of AvatarSMP.

Your information is not collected for advertising or sold to third parties.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

WhiteLister uses a local SQLite database to store application records, whitelist administration records and relevant moderation information.

Access to administrative information is restricted to authorised members of the AvatarSMP moderation team.

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information against unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet-based service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Third-Party Services and the AvatarSMP API

WhiteLister communicates with Discord through the Discord API to provide its functionality.

It also communicates with the AvatarSMP server API to perform whitelist operations.

When a moderator approves or removes a player, the relevant Minecraft gamertag is sent to the AvatarSMP API so that the server allowlist can be updated.

The API may also provide server information and online player information to support WhiteLister's commands.

Information is shared only where necessary to provide the bot's functionality and administer AvatarSMP.

Discord and AvatarSMP's infrastructure may process information under their own applicable privacy policies and terms.

## 6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for application management, moderation, security and server administration.

Application and moderation records may be retained to maintain an accurate history of decisions and administrative actions.

Users may contact the AvatarSMP administration team to request information about their stored data or ask for its deletion.

Some records may need to be retained where there is a legitimate administrative or security reason to do so.

## 7. Data Sharing

WhiteLister does not sell, rent or trade personal information.

Information may be accessed by authorised AvatarSMP moderators and administrators where necessary to manage applications, moderation and server access.

Information may also be processed by Discord and the infrastructure providers supporting the service.

We do not intentionally make application or moderation records publicly available.

## 8. Your Rights and Data Requests

Depending on applicable data protection law, you may have the right to:

- Request access to personal information held about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of personal information.
- Object to certain types of processing.
- Ask questions about how your information is used.

To make a data request, contact the AvatarSMP administration team through the official Discord server.

Requests will be considered in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

## 9. Children's Privacy

WhiteLister is designed to support the AvatarSMP community and its application process.

Users should comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and any additional age restrictions established by AvatarSMP.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as WhiteLister develops or its functionality changes.

Any significant changes will be reflected in the published version of this document.

## 11. Contact

For questions, concerns or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the information processed by WhiteLister, contact the AvatarSMP administration team through the official AvatarSMP Discord server.
